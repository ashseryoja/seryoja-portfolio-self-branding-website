## How I think about AI engineering
My job is to make probabilistic models behave predictably inside deterministic products:
design for provider outages, validate model output before it reaches users, track the cost
of every call, and measure quality instead of assuming it. Most of the work is solid
backend engineering around the model — queues, retries, idempotency, migrations, tests and
observability.
